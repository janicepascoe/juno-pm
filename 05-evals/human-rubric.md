## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno’s PI Objective Status Review
- **Value proposition:** Review the PI work against objectives and key results to give an early indication of where the team may need to respond to protect intended Business Value. Juno will highlight work that does not align to an existing objective.  The result is a summary for each objective which contains suggested percent complete and a recommendation of potential new or updated objective for PM consideration.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy (deal-breaker) | Does each PI objective note the source material that it made it's conclusion from? | Yes / No | At least one item cannot be traced back to an artifact. | n/a | At least one ADO entry should show for the PI objective and maybe some other artifacts if the objective is started. An actual artifact is used for each result |
| Sources | Does the result information look like it accurately depicts the source? | Yes / No | One or more results has no source, or the link is wrong. | n/a | Every result has a working link to its source. |
| Prioritization | Order objectives by BV (highest to lowest) and prioritize objectives not started or not information could be found | 1 to 5 | Misordered or missing objectives | Work fell into the wrong objectives or was orphaned when it belonged under an objective | All objectives are appropriately stated with appropriate work indicated under each one.   Ordered by BV Highlighting those perceived not started or with the least work started. |
| Usefulness | Could I act on this summary without rereading the sources? | 1 to 5 | I have to reread, reorganize or redo the whole summary. | Clear enough to act on, but I still check one or two sources. | I can act on it straight away and forward it to my team. |
| Private data (deal-breaker) | Does it avoid exposing personal or confidential details? | Yes / No | Includes names, PHI, or has wording that could be negatively perceived by the team. | n/a | No names, no PHI, does not call out blame on team members |
| Alignment detection | Juno correctly identifies work that is materially misaligned with a PI objective and supports the finding with traceable evidence. | 1 to 5 | Incorrectly identifies aligned work as misaligned or misses obvious material drift. | Directionally correct, but evidence or reasoning is incomplete. | Correctly identifies material misalignment, clearly explains why, and cites strong supporting evidence. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** Once a week in prep for Monday DSUs
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, another PM and an RTE
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** Our scores should be in sync - we would come together to agree each time.

## Pass bar

An average of 4 or higher on prioritization, usefulness and alignment scores. as well as every yes or no question is scored a yes.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Accuracy: any "no"
- Private data: any "no"
- Any unsupported material alignment claim, tool access failure, and anything determined to be a hallucination.
